\section*{Problem 1}

Pick a box uniformly at random. Box 1 has 2 white, 3 blue. Box 2 has 4 white, 1 blue.

\[
\Pr(B_1\mid \text{blue})
=\frac{\Pr(\text{blue}\mid B_1)\Pr(B_1)}{\Pr(\text{blue})}
=\frac{\frac{3}{5}\cdot \frac12}{\frac12\cdot\frac{3}{5}+\frac12\cdot\frac{1}{5}}
=\frac{3/10}{4/10}=\frac34.
\]
