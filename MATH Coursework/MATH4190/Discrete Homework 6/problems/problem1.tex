\section*{Problem 1 — Conventional Polynomial Evaluation}

We are given the conventional algorithm:
\begin{align*}
\textbf{procedure } \mathrm{POLYNOMIAL}(c, a_0, a_1, \ldots, a_n : \text{ real numbers}) \\
\quad power \leftarrow 1 \\
\quad y \leftarrow a_0 \\
\quad \textbf{for } i = 1 \text{ to } n \textbf{ do} \\
\qquad power \leftarrow power \cdot c \\
\qquad y \leftarrow y + a_i \cdot power \\
\quad \textbf{end for} \\
\quad \textbf{return } y
\end{align*}

\subsection*{(a) Evaluate $3x^2 + x + 1$ at $x=2$ by tracing each step}
Here $n=2$, $c=2$, and $(a_0,a_1,a_2)=(1,1,3)$. The table logs every assignment step.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c}
\textbf{Step} & \textbf{Action} & \textbf{power} & \textbf{y} \\
\hline
1 & $power \leftarrow 1$ & $1$ & -- \\
2 & $y \leftarrow a_0$ & $1$ & $1$ \\
\hline
3 & $i=1$: $power \leftarrow power\cdot c = 1\cdot 2$ & $2$ & $1$ \\
4 & $i=1$: $y \leftarrow y + a_1\cdot power = 1 + 1\cdot 2$ & $2$ & $3$ \\
\hline
5 & $i=2$: $power \leftarrow power\cdot c = 2\cdot 2$ & $4$ & $3$ \\
6 & $i=2$: $y \leftarrow y + a_2\cdot power = 3 + 3\cdot 4$ & $4$ & $15$ \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Return: } $y=15$. Thus $3(2)^2 + 2 + 1 = 15$.

\subsection*{(b) Exact operation counts}
Initialization ($power\leftarrow 1$, $y\leftarrow a_0$) performs no additions or multiplications.

Each loop iteration ($i=1,\dots,n$) performs:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $power \leftarrow power\cdot c$: \textbf{1 multiplication}
  \item $y \leftarrow y + a_i\cdot power$: \textbf{1 multiplication} and \textbf{1 addition}
\end{itemize}

Therefore, over $n$ iterations:
\[
\boxed{\text{Multiplications} = 2n} 
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\boxed{\text{Additions} = n}.
\]
