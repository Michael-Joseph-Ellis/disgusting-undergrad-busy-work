\section*{Problem 2}

Power generator with \(p=7\), \(d=3\), seed \(x_0=2\), and
\[
x_{n+1}\equiv x_n^{\,d}\pmod p.
\]

Compute:
\[
x_1\equiv 2^3\equiv 8\equiv 1\pmod 7,\qquad
x_2\equiv 1^3\equiv 1\pmod 7,
\]
and then \(x_n\equiv 1\) for all \(n\ge 2\).

So the pseudorandom sequence is
\[
\boxed{x_1=1,\ x_2=1,\ x_3=1,\ \ldots}
\]
(with the seed \(x_0=2\)).
