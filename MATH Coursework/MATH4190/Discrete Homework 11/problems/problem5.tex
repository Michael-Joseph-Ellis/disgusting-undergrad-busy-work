\section*{Problem 5}

Decrypt the messages encrypted with the shift cipher \(f(p)=(p+10)\bmod 26\).
Decryption shifts back by 10.

\begin{enumerate}[(a)]
\item \(\texttt{CEBBOXNOB XYG} \;\to\; \boxed{\text{SURRENDER NOW}}\).
\item \(\texttt{LO WI PBSOXN} \;\to\; \boxed{\text{BE MY FRIEND}}\).
\item \(\texttt{DSWO PYB PEX} \;\to\; \boxed{\text{TIME FOR FUN}}\).
\end{enumerate}
