\section*{Problem 3}

Binary symmetric channel with crossover probability \(p<\tfrac12\).
Code
\[
C=\{00000,\,11100,\,00111,\,11011\},
\]
received word \(y=00100\).

For a codeword \(x\), \(\Pr(y\mid x)\) on a BSC is
\[
\Pr(y\mid x)=p^{d(x,y)}(1-p)^{5-d(x,y)},
\]
where \(d(x,y)\) is the Hamming distance.

\subsection*{(a) Probabilities}

Distances:
\[
\begin{aligned}
d(00000,00100)&=1,\\
d(11100,00100)&=2,\\
d(00111,00100)&=2,\\
d(11011,00100)&=5.
\end{aligned}
\]
So
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Pr(y\mid 00000)&=p(1-p)^4,\\
\Pr(y\mid 11100)&=p^2(1-p)^3,\\
\Pr(y\mid 00111)&=p^2(1-p)^3,\\
\Pr(y\mid 11011)&=p^5.
\end{aligned}
\]

\subsection*{(b) Maximum likelihood estimate}

Because \(p<\tfrac12\), the probability is largest for the smallest distance.  
The unique closest codeword is \(00000\) with distance \(1\).  

So the ML estimate of the transmitted word is
\[
\boxed{00000}.
\]
