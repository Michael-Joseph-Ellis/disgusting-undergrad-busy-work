\section*{Problem 8}

Random variable \(X\) takes values in \(\mathcal{A}=\{A,B,C,D,E\}\) with
\[
\Pr(A)=0.40,\quad
\Pr(B)=0.20,\quad
\Pr(C)=0.20,\quad
\Pr(D)=0.10,\quad
\Pr(E)=0.10.
\]

\subsection*{(a) Shannon entropy}

\[
\begin{aligned}
H(X) &= -\sum_{x} p(x)\log_2 p(x) \\
&= 0.4\log_2\frac{1}{0.4}
  + 0.2\log_2\frac{1}{0.2}
  + 0.2\log_2\frac{1}{0.2}
  + 0.1\log_2\frac{1}{0.1}
  + 0.1\log_2\frac{1}{0.1}\\
&\approx 0.4(1.3219)+2\cdot 0.2(2.3219)+2\cdot 0.1(3.3219)\\
&\approx 2.12\ \text{bits}.
\end{aligned}
\]

\subsection*{(b) Huffman code and average length}

Probabilities: \(0.40,0.20,0.20,0.10,0.10\).

Huffman steps:

1. Combine \(D(0.10)\) and \(E(0.10)\Rightarrow DE(0.20)\).
2. Combine \(B(0.20)\) and \(C(0.20)\Rightarrow BC(0.40)\).
3. Combine \(DE(0.20)\) and \(A(0.40)\Rightarrow ADE(0.60)\).
4. Combine \(ADE(0.60)\) and \(BC(0.40)\Rightarrow 1.0\).

Assign \(0/1\) arbitrarily at each split, e.g.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{Symbol} & \text{Codeword} \\ \hline
A & 01\\
B & 10\\
C & 11\\
D & 000\\
E & 001
\end{array}
\]

Average code length:
\[
\begin{aligned}
L &= 0.4\cdot 2 + 0.2\cdot 2 + 0.2\cdot 2 + 0.1\cdot 3 + 0.1\cdot 3 \\
  &= 1.6 + 0.6 = 2.2\ \text{bits}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Check the Shannon bounds:
\[
H(X)\approx 2.12 \le L = 2.2 < H(X)+1 \approx 3.12.
\]
