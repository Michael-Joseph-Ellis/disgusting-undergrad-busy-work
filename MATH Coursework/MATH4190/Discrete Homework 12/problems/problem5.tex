Here $n=p=1093$ is prime, $e=361$. Then $\varphi(n)=n-1=1092$ and Eve should take
\[
d\equiv e^{-1}\pmod{1092}.
\]
Euclid: $1092=3\cdot 361+9$, $361=40\cdot 9+1$, so
\[
1=361-40(1092-3\cdot 361)=121\cdot 361-40\cdot 1092.
\]
Hence $d\equiv \boxed{121}\pmod{1092}$, and Eve decrypts by $m\equiv c^{121}\ (\bmod\ 1093)$.
