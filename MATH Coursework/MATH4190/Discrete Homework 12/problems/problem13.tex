\section*{Problem 13}

We need a Shamir scheme where:
- The general alone can launch,
- Or the two colonels together,
- Or all five clerks together,
- But no smaller group can.

Use a \((1,1)\), \((2,2)\), and \((5,5)\) structure combined under a higher global threshold.

Construct a \((1,10)\) Shamir scheme:
- Give the general share \((0,s)\) directly (he can recover alone).
- For the two colonels, embed the secret into a \((2,2)\) subsystem,
  distribute both shares among them.
- For the clerks, embed into a \((5,5)\) subsystem,
  distribute the five shares.

All these subschemes encode the same secret.  
Any authorized group gets all shares of at least one full subsystem; any unauthorized group does not.

Thus the launch conditions are satisfied.
