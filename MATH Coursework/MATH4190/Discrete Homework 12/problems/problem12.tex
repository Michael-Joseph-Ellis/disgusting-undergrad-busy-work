\section*{Problem 12}

A \((3,5)\) Shamir scheme over \(p=17\). Shares:
\[
(1,8),\ (3,10),\ (5,11).
\]

Use Lagrange interpolation to compute \(f(0)\).

\[
f(0)
= 8\cdot \ell_1(0)+10\cdot \ell_3(0)+11\cdot \ell_5(0) \pmod{17}.
\]

Compute basis terms:
\[
\ell_1(0)=\frac{(0-3)(0-5)}{(1-3)(1-5)}
= \frac{(-3)(-5)}{(-2)(-4)}
= \frac{15}{8}\equiv 15\cdot 8^{-1}\equiv 15\cdot 15\equiv 4,
\]

\[
\ell_3(0)=\frac{(0-1)(0-5)}{(3-1)(3-5)}
=\frac{(-1)(-5)}{(2)(-2)}
=\frac{5}{-4}\equiv 5\cdot (-4)^{-1}
=5\cdot (-4)^{-1}.
\]
Since \((-4)\equiv 13\), \(13^{-1}\equiv 4\).  
Thus \(\ell_3(0)=5\cdot 4=20\equiv 3\).

\[
\ell_5(0)=\frac{(0-1)(0-3)}{(5-1)(5-3)}
= \frac{(-1)(-3)}{(4)(2)}
= \frac{3}{8}\equiv 3\cdot 8^{-1}\equiv 3\cdot 15=45\equiv 11.
\]

Now compute:
\[
f(0)\equiv 8\cdot 4 + 10\cdot 3 + 11\cdot 11
=32+30+121=183\equiv \boxed{13}\pmod{17}.
\]

So the secret is \(\boxed{13}\).
