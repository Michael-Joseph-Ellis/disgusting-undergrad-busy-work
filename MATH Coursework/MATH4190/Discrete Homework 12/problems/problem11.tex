\section*{Problem 11}

A \((2,10)\) Shamir scheme over prime \(73\). Two known shares:
\[
(1,10),\qquad (2,18).
\]

Polynomial is linear: \(f(x)=ax+b\pmod{73}\).  
From \(f(1)=10\) we get \(a+b\equiv 10\).  
From \(f(2)=18\) we get \(2a+b\equiv 18\).

Subtract to find \(a\equiv 8\).  
Then \(b\equiv 10-8=2\).

Thus
\[
f(x)=8x+2\pmod{73}.
\]

The third share at \(x=5\) is:
\[
f(5)=8\cdot 5+2=42.
\]
So the missing value is
\[
\boxed{42}.
\]
