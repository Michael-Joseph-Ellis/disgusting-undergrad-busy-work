\section*{Problem 10}

Find the number of trailing zeros in $100!$.

\textbf{Solution.}
The number of zeros is the exponent of $10=2\cdot 5$ in $100!$, which is
$\min\{v_2(100!), v_5(100!)\} = v_5(100!)$.

\[
v_5(100!)=\left\lfloor \frac{100}{5}\right\rfloor
+\left\lfloor \frac{100}{25}\right\rfloor
+\left\lfloor \frac{100}{125}\right\rfloor
=20+4+0=24.
\]

So $100!$ ends with $\boxed{24}$ zeros.
