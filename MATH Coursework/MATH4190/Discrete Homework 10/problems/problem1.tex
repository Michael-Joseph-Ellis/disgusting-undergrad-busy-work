\section*{Problem 1}

What time does a 12-hour clock read:

\begin{enumerate}[(a)]
\item 80 hours after 11{:}00?
\item 40 hours before 12{:}00?
\item 100 hours after 6{:}00?
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Solution.} Reduce offsets modulo 12 hours.

\begin{enumerate}[(a)]
\item $80 \equiv 8 \pmod{12}$, so $11{:}00+8$ hours $=19{:}00\equiv \boxed{7{:}00}$.
\item $40 \equiv 4 \pmod{12}$, so 40 hours before is 4 hours before: $12{:}00-4=\boxed{8{:}00}$.
\item $100 \equiv 4 \pmod{12}$, so $6{:}00+4=\boxed{10{:}00}$.
\end{enumerate}
