\section*{Problem 6}

Prove that the product of any three consecutive integers is divisible by $6$.

\textbf{Solution.}
Let the integers be $n,n+1,n+2$.

At least one of them is even, so the product is divisible by $2$.
Also, one of $n,n+1,n+2$ is divisible by $3$ (pigeonhole principle modulo $3$).
Therefore the product is divisible by both $2$ and $3$, hence by $\mathrm{lcm}(2,3)=6$.
