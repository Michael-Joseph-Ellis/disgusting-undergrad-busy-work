Part 5 Talking Points
=====================
Dataset size used for exploration: 735 respondents

Main visual patterns to mention:
- Daily music listening time has a wide spread, so respondents vary a lot in how much they listen.
- Mental health scores are spread across the 0-10 scale, which supports comparison across respondents.
- The scatter plots help check whether more listening time appears related to anxiety, depression, insomnia, or OCD.
- Boxplots by Music Effects show whether respondents who say music improves their mental health report different scores.
- Genre counts show which music tastes are most common in the sample.

Ethical interpretation reminders:
- Avoid claiming causation from these plots alone.
- Do not overgeneralize to all music listeners.
- Mention that the data are self-reported and come from a survey sample.
